# Privacy Policy

_Effective October 8, 2026_

This policy explains what fuwa.chat, the fuwa instance run by Waifu Devs ("we", "us"), keeps about you, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. Other fuwa instances are run by other people and have their own policies.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other sites.

## What we keep

**Your account.** Your username, display name, password (stored only as a salted hash, never in the clear), profile (picture, banner, bio, status, pronouns and anything else you add to it), your settings, and your two-step sign-in secrets if you turn it on. If you sign in with waifu.dev, Google, X or Twitch, we keep that service's ID for you and your name there, never your email or their tokens. fuwa.chat doesn't ask for your email address.

**Your devices.** For each signed-in device: when it signed in, when it was last used, and the app or browser it reported, so you can see and sign out your devices in Settings.

**What you post in servers.** Messages, reactions, files, pictures, emoji, polls, threads and edits, along with which servers you're in, your roles and your server profiles. They're stored on our servers so the members who can see each channel can read them. Votes in anonymous polls are kept in a way that doesn't say who voted.

**Direct messages.** Direct messages and secure channels are end-to-end encrypted on your devices. We only keep the encrypted messages, which we can't read, plus what we need to deliver them: who's in each conversation, which devices they use, those devices' public keys, and when each message was sent. Calls in direct messages are encrypted the same way.

**Friends.** Your friends, friend requests and blocks, and your settings for them. Nobody else is told about them, and a block isn't shown to the person you blocked.

**Calls.** Voice and video in server channels go through our media servers so they reach the others in the call. They aren't recorded unless someone the server allows to record turns it on; everyone in the call sees it and hears a beep when it starts. Recordings are kept on our servers for the people who may record in that channel, until they delete them. Who is in a call is kept only in memory, while it lasts.

**Presence.** Whether you're online and what you've chosen to share as your activity is kept only in memory, and you can turn it off in Settings.

**Your IP address.** Our servers and our hosting provider see your IP address when your app connects, and use it to send you data and to protect the service from abuse. We don't keep it with your account. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived request logs that include it.

**On your device.** The app stores your sign-in, settings and encryption keys in your browser's storage or the desktop app's files. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Anonymous statistics

fuwa.chat sends Waifu Devs anonymous usage and health reports: counts, timings and error types, with no content, names, IP addresses or IDs of people or servers. The apps also send anonymous error reports, which you can turn off in Settings under "Help fix bugs".

## Who else sees it

- **People on fuwa.chat.** Your profile is visible to people who share a server with you. What you post in a channel is visible to everyone who can see that channel, including server moderators, and to agents (bots) and integrations the server's managers added. Server owners and moderators also see what's in the server's audit log.
- **Other instances.** If a server shares a channel with a server on another fuwa instance, what you post in that channel, and your name and picture, are sent to that instance and its members.
- **Hosting and storage.** We run fuwa.chat on Railway, in the United States and, for some servers, in Europe. Our backups are stored with the same provider, in the same regions.
- **GIF search.** When you search for GIFs, our server sends your search words to our GIF provider. It doesn't see your IP address or your account.
- **Moderation services.** If a server turns on AutoMod's Smart filter, the text of messages posted there is sent to the moderation service its managers picked, to be checked. Mentions and IDs are taken out first.
- **Sign-in services.** When you sign in with waifu.dev, Google, X or Twitch, you go to their site, which sees your IP address and handles your sign-in under its own privacy policy.
- **Pictures from other sites.** Pictures linked from other sites are fetched by our server and passed on to you, so those sites don't learn your IP address.
- **Feedback.** If you send feedback to @fuwafuwa, your words (without names, IDs or mentions) are sent to Anthropic's Claude to sort, and may be posted publicly as GitHub issues in fuwa's repository.
- **The law.** We may share information when the law requires it, or when it's needed to protect someone's safety or our users and service. We'll only share what we have and what's needed; we can't share the content of end-to-end encrypted messages.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and what you post until you delete them. Deleting a message removes it. Deleting your account removes your profile, pictures, devices, sessions, encryption keys and friends. Messages you posted in servers stay there, shown as from "Deleted account", so conversations still make sense; delete them first if you want them gone. Encrypted direct messages stay with the other people in the conversation, who can still read them. Deleted data can remain in our backups for a limited time.

## Your rights

You can:

- **see and download** everything we keep about you, from Settings, Your account, Data;
- **change** your profile and settings at any time;
- **delete** your messages and your account, from Settings;
- **object** to how we use your data, or ask us to limit it;
- **complain** to your local data protection authority.

We use your data to run the service you asked for (the contract between us), to keep it safe and working (our legitimate interest), and where the law requires it. For anything else, write to us at {contact} and we'll answer within 30 days.

Your data is stored in the United States, and for some servers in Europe. If you're outside those places, it's transferred there to run the service.

## Children

fuwa.chat isn't for children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where they live. If we learn we hold an account of someone younger, we delete it.

## Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted with HTTPS, direct messages are end-to-end encrypted, and you can turn on two-step sign-in. No service is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## Changes

We may update this policy. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they apply.
